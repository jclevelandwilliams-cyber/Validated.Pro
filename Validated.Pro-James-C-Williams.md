## What This Is

This repository is the public distribution layer for Validated Field Notes, a market intelligence report series tracking capital flow, institutional positioning, strategic acquisitions, and enterprise movement.

Built for decision-makers who cannot afford narrative distortion, weak sourcing, or market theater.

---

## What Gets Published Here

**Validated Field Notes (VFN)**
Structured intelligence reports covering enterprise AI, private equity activity, capital structure, and strategic positioning. Each report identifies the gap between what was announced and what the record shows.

**Market Research & Validation**
Independent analysis for founders, operators, and investors who need to know whether a market exists and whether it can be won before committing capital to it.

**Document Clarity**
Contracts, agreements, filings, and disclosures translated into plain English.

---

## Methodology

**Dual Lens Analytics™**
Two-pass verification methodology separating source aggregation from independent review.

**ESPoint Matrix™**
Cross-reference scoring framework applied across all published reports.

Reports are built on primary source research, cross-referenced across a minimum of three independent institutional confirmations before publication. No single source determines outcome.
