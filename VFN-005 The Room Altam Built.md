*Source: NVIDIA 10-Q filed April 28, 2026 · Five point drop in one quarter*

Huang stood at GTC in March 2026 and told the room NVIDIA had Chinese purchase orders for H200 chips and manufacturing was restarting. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick said in April no H200 chips had actually been sold to China yet.

A Commerce Department rule published yesterday extended export controls to any chip with die-to-die interconnect bandwidth above 600 GB/s. The H200 runs at 900. The B100 runs at 1.2 terabytes per second. Both captured.

Before that rule landed Chinese firms including ByteDance, Tencent, and Alibaba were already reaching out to Huawei for Ascend 950 orders. DeepSeek optimized V4 specifically for Huawei chips. The revenue was already falling before anyone published a rule about it.

> Zero completed sales. Five point revenue drop. One quarter. The 10-Q was already showing it.
